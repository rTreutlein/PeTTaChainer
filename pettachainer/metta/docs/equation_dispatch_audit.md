# Repeated equation-head audit

This audit covers top-level production `.metta` files in `pettachainer/metta`.
Tests, examples, benchmarks, linter fixtures and `piPLN_paper_explained` are
excluded from the production inventory. Patterns were compared by unification,
not source order. A repeated head is acceptable only when its clauses are
constructor/arity-disjoint, a unifying fallback is explicitly guarded to emit
`empty`, or the operation deliberately enumerates alternatives.
